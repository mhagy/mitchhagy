Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Who we are

This website, mghagy.com, is operated by M.G. Hagy doing business as MGH Consultation, based in Grand Junction, Colorado. Questions about this policy can be sent to advisory@mghagy.com.

Information you share with us

When you use the consultation request form, your device's email application opens a pre-filled message addressed to us. We receive only what you choose to send in that email, such as your name, email address, business stage and message. This site does not store form entries on any server.

How we use your information

We use the information you send solely to respond to your inquiry, schedule a conversation and provide consulting services you request. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Information collected automatically

This site does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Like most websites, our hosting provider (Vercel) may record basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested in server logs for security and reliability. The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google when you visit.

Confidentiality

Anything you share about your business in an inquiry or consultation is treated as confidential and is not disclosed to third parties without your permission, except where required by law.

Data retention

We keep correspondence only as long as needed to respond to you, provide services and meet any legal or tax obligations. You can ask us to delete your correspondence at any time.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by emailing advisory@mghagy.com.

Children

This site is intended for business owners and founders and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
